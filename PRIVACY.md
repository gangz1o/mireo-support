# 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

**生效日期 / Effective Date:** 2026-11-23

---

## 简体中文

觅乐（Mireo）高度重视你的隐私。本应用遵循最小化数据原则设计：

1. **不追踪、无广告**  
   觅乐不包含任何第三方广告 SDK，也不会出于广告或跨应用追踪目的收集你的个人信息。
2. **本地存储与凭据安全**  
   - 你的播放偏好、外观设置、缓存及本地音乐库索引仅保存在你的设备上。
   - 你添加的 NAS、WebDAV 或音乐服务器登录凭据与密钥均安全存储在系统钥匙串（Keychain）中，仅限本机访问，不会上传至开发者服务器。
3. **iCloud 同步（可选）**  
   如果你开启了 iCloud 同步，你的收藏、自建歌单、歌词时间偏移、音效预设与外观主题将通过 Apple 的 iCloud / CloudKit 在你自己的设备之间同步。开发者无法访问你在 iCloud 中的私有数据。
4. **外部服务请求**  
   当你主动使用 Apple Music、连接自己的 NAS / 自托管服务器、使用在线歌词匹配（如 LRCLIB）或配置自定义音乐服务时，相关请求会直接发送至你所选择的服务端，并受对应服务条款与隐私政策约束。
5. **联系与支持**  
   如对隐私政策有任何疑问，请通过 [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/gangz1o/mireo-support/issues) 与我们联系。

---

## English

Mireo respects your privacy and is designed with data minimization in mind:

1. **No Tracking, No Ads**  
   Mireo does not include third-party advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.
2. **On-Device Storage & Credential Security**  
   - Your playback preferences, appearance settings, caches, and local library index remain on your device.
   - Credentials and tokens for your NAS, WebDAV, or music servers are stored securely in the system Keychain on your device and are never sent to the developer.
3. **iCloud Sync (Optional)**  
   When enabled, your favorites, playlists, lyrics timing offsets, audio presets, and appearance themes sync across your own Apple devices using Apple's iCloud / CloudKit. The developer has no access to your private iCloud data.
4. **User-Selected Services**  
   When you choose to connect Apple Music, your NAS or self-hosted music server, online lyrics lookup (such as LRCLIB), or custom music services, requests are sent directly to the services you configure and are governed by their respective privacy policies.
5. **Contact & Support**  
   If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/gangz1o/mireo-support/issues).
